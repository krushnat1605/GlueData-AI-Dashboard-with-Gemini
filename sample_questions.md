# Sample Questions

Try these questions in the **AWS Glue AI Copilot** after uploading the sample dataset.

## Overall Analytics

- How many executions are there?
- How many jobs succeeded?
- How many jobs failed?
- What is the success rate?
- What is the failure rate?
- How many unique jobs are there?

## Cost Analytics

- What is the total cost?
- What is the average cost?
- Which job has the highest cost?
- Show the top 5 expensive jobs.
- Show cost by application.
- Show cost by business unit.
- Show cost by environment.

## Failure Analytics

- How many executions failed?
- Show failed jobs.
- Which job failed the most?
- Show failures by application.
- Show failures by business unit.
- Show failures by environment.
- Which application has the most failures?

## Waste Analytics

- What is the total failed cost?
- What is the wasted runtime?
- What is the wasted DPU?
- Show failed cost by application.
- Show failed cost by business unit.

## Runtime Analytics

- What is the total runtime?
- What is the average runtime?
- Which job has the longest runtime?
- Show the top 5 longest-running jobs.
- Show runtime by application.
- Show runtime by business unit.

## DPU Analytics

- What is the total DPU usage?
- What is the average DPU usage?
- Which job uses the most DPU?
- Show DPU usage by application.
- Show DPU usage by business unit.
- Show DPU usage by environment.
